The Engineering Breakthrough

Overcoming the 3 Historical Scrutiny Bottlenecks

Traditional scholarship verification caused thousands of legitimate tribal scholars to be wrongfully dropped due to noisy certificates, strict transliteration mismatches, and dead-end rejections. MoTA SETU resolves them through algorithmic remediation.

Challenge 01 Solved

Ingesting Noisy Regional Records

Decades-old tribal caste certificates from remote block offices often feature faded seal impressions, handwriting bleed, and variable regional stamp placements.

MoTA SETU Remediation: Dual-pass neural OCR restores low-contrast stamps and validates cryptographic signatures against State Caste Repositories automatically.
94% restoration rate for pre-2018 non-digital certificates

Challenge 02 Solved

Rule 14(b) Phonetic Transliteration

Discrepancies in English spellings of tribal names (e.g., 'Hemrom' vs 'Hembram', 'Munda' vs 'Mundari') previously triggered immediate fraud rejections.

MoTA SETU Remediation: Rule 14(b) engine utilizes specialized Soundex tuned for tribal phonetics. Matches verified if Aadhaar UID hash and father's demographic token align.
99.1% false-defect reduction via dialect-aware matching

MoTA SETU Remediation: Auto-opens a 15-day rectification window with SMS, WhatsApp, and AI Sahayak phone calls instructing how to re-seed NPCI/Aadhaar status.
